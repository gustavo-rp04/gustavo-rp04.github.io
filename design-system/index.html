Design system · v1.0

Midnight & Coral

A small, editorial design system for personal brands and professional-services sites: deep midnight navy for authority, one coral highlight for energy, generous whitespace and a single geometric sans. Copy tokens.css and components.css into a new project, change the tokens, and every component follows.

Foundations

Color

Navy carries authority, coral is the only bright accent and is used sparingly: highlighted words, primary calls to action on dark, badges, numbers. Text on coral is always ink, never white. Body text on the light background passes WCAG AA at 8:1.

Foundations

Typography

One family, DM Sans, at regular weight with tight tracking for headings: modern and calm rather than loud. JetBrains Mono is reserved for small uppercase labels (eyebrows, dates, counters), a quiet nod to data and code. Sizes are fluid with clamp(), so they scale from phone to desktop without breakpoints.

Space & shape

An 8-based spacing scale, a 1280px container with fluid side gutters, and fluid section spacing (80→144px). Radii: 6px for images, 10px for tiles, 20px for large surfaces, pill for buttons and chips. Every interactive target is at least 44px.

Components

Buttons

Primary (ink) for the main action on light backgrounds, outline for the secondary action next to it, accent (coral) for the one action that must stand out, such as Download CV or Send. Buttons lift slightly on hover. Round icon buttons are for arrows, menu and back-to-top.

Patterns

Section head

Every section opens the same way: a mono eyebrow in a narrow left column, an h2 with one highlighted phrase, and an optional one-line intro. An action (like Download CV) can sit at the right of the title with .section-head__row. Highlight one phrase per heading, never more.

Service accordion

For offers, capabilities or case studies. Collapsed, a row shows a coral icon disc (simple 1.6px-stroke line icons: compass, database, people, cap, microphone), the title with key-word chips under it, and a one-line description, so a whole list fits on one screen. Clicking a row opens its examples. It's built on native <details>, so it works without JavaScript and with the keyboard. On phones, the collapsed row shows only the number and title.

Scroll timeline

For experience, education or project history. A vertical line fills with coral as the visitor scrolls, and each dot lights up when reached (main.js sets --progress). Each entry is collapsed to its date, role and organization, and the details open on click (native <details>).

Foundations

Motion

Motion is quiet and purposeful: content fades up 24px as it enters (700ms, ease-out, staggered with --reveal-delay); highlighter strokes draw from left to right (900ms); stats count up (1.4s); buttons lift 2px on hover (160ms). Everything is switched off when the visitor prefers reduced motion, and content stays visible if JavaScript is off.

--ease-out: cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1) --duration-fast: 160ms --duration-base: 320ms --duration-slow: 700ms --duration-draw: 900ms
